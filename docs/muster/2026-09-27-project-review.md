# Muster project review, 2026-09-27

Whole-repo review of `muster` at `8ad753801f` (v1.14.29) plus the uncommitted reset-credits work. Seven read-only reviewers each covered one area: Sites and the muster-sites MCP, chat mode, integrations (Extension Hub, ActiveCollab, usage, updater), main-process core and security, renderer, build/CI/tests, and agentic features with upstream drift. Findings were then merged and ranked here.

How to read the tables:

- Severity: H high, M medium, L low.
- Effort: S under 1 hour, M about half a day, L several days.
- Evidence: `code` means the reviewer traced the path end to end, `test` means a scratch test reproduced it, `suspected` means the mechanism is plausible but unconfirmed. A dagger (†) marks items re-checked by hand while writing this report.
- Items already delivered in the 2026-08-03 roadmap are not repeated. Section 9 gives the status of its open items.

## 7. Upstream drift and sync strategy

Upstream landed 2,321 commits in the 36 days to 2026-08-30 (the last local fetch), about 64 a day across 16,746 files. Muster has changed 3,172 files: 1,308 are new Muster-only files (about two thirds of the diff) and 1,371 are files both sides changed. Upstream has since deleted or gutted 221 files Muster edits: `shared/types.ts` was split and its imports rewritten across ~2,300 files, `persistence.ts` became a re-export, `TaskPage.tsx` went from 523 KB to 94 KB. Only 2 of the 12 ports in the 2026-08-31 port plan have landed, and Muster's history has no recorded cherry-picks.

A full merge or rebase is no longer realistic. Recommended approach:

1. Two cherry-pick lanes. Weekly (30-60 min): upstream fixes for security, crashes, data loss, leaks and hangs, filtered by subject grep. Monthly: perf and feature items chosen by value, done as guided reimplementations, as in the 2026-08-31 plan.
2. A ledger file of upstream hashes marked ported, dropped or blocked, so triage isn't repeated.
3. Shrink future conflicts: new Muster work goes in Muster-owned modules; edits to upstream hot files stay at one import plus one call; Muster strings move to an overlay catalog merged at load (`i18n/i18n.ts:9`), since the five locale files are the most reliable conflict source. Move the 22 ActiveCollab imports and methods Muster added to `orca-runtime.ts:704-746` into a `RuntimeActiveCollabCommands` class.
4. Re-fork (fresh upstream, drop in the Muster-only files, redo ~1,500 small edits, roughly 2-4 weeks) only if a must-have upstream feature can't be ported.
